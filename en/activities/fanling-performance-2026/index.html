About the activity

2026

On 16 January 2026, students brought ensemble works in different styles to the Fanling community, sharing the results of their regular training through bold costumes, rhythmic movement and stage formations.

The street-dance crew used inverted skills, standing movement and grounded shapes to create dynamic levels. The community performance also developed practical experience in waiting, transitions, audience awareness and supporting teammates.
