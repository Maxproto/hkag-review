About the activity

2002

In December 2002, Hong Kong Art Groupe students travelled to Sichuan for a cultural exchange and visited the natural and historic surroundings of the Anlan Suspension Bridge.

Woodland, the historic bridge and traditional architecture introduced students to local culture through first-hand experience. The group journey also developed curiosity, independence and care for fellow travellers.
