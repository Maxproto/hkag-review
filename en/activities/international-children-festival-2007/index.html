Hong Kong International Children’s Cultural Arts Festival

The festival connected children’s groups from Beijing and Hong Kong through performance and exchange, with the Sai Kung programme held on 22 July 2007.

About the activity

2007

The 2007 Hong Kong International Children’s Cultural Arts Festival travelled to Beijing Haidian Theatre, Sai Kung Jockey Club Town Hall and North District Town Hall, bringing young performers from different places together on shared stages.

Bright costumes, ensemble formations and theatre lighting gave the programme variety and depth. The multi-group festival also allowed students to discover different works and broaden their cultural perspective through performance and exchange.
