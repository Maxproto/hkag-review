About the activity

2007

In July 2007, Hong Kong Art Groupe travelled to Beijing for tenth-anniversary cultural activities and an arts festival, sharing stages, discovering new work and joining city visits with local children’s arts groups.

Multiple groups brought distinctive costumes and works to the programme. Sharing the stage allowed young performers to appreciate one another’s training and learn respect for schedules, spaces and collaborators.
