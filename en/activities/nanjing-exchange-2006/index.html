About the activity

2006

In 2006, Hong Kong Art Groupe travelled to Nanjing for a cultural arts exchange performance, sharing an ensemble work with local audiences and arts groups.

Dancers in bright green used tilting, extension and broad formations to create continuous movement. Performing in another city taught students to adapt to new stage dimensions, lighting and schedules while discovering varied forms of artistic expression.
