04 · Groove · Creativity · Crew

Street Dance

Start with groove, then find your own style within the crew.

Groove · Creativity · Crew

Foundational groove, timing, coordination, strength and creative practice lead into combinations, formations and performance.

Who may enjoy this direction?

For learners who enjoy strong beats, exploration and crew choreography, and want to develop creativity on safe foundations.

Training focus

Begin with foundations and grow towards complete expression.

  1. 01

    Foundational groove, timing and weight

  2. 02

    Coordination, strength and control

  3. 03

    Formations, interaction and short choreography

  4. 04

    Individual style and stage confidence

What learning develops

A class builds more than movement.

  • Responding to music with the whole body
  • Maintaining spacing and awareness within a crew
  • Exploring creative movement and style safely

Class and stage

From daily practice to performance, every step is part of a student’s growth.
