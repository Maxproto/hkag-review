02 · Line · Music · Focus

Ballet

Build a body that is precise, musical, poised and strong.

Line · Music · Focus

Progressive barre and centre work, musicality and stage practice develop posture, focus, control and expressive detail.

Who may enjoy this direction?

For learners who enjoy structured practice, music and movement detail, and who are ready to build technique through consistency.

Training focus

Begin with foundations and grow towards complete expression.

  1. 01

    Posture, placement and classical positions

  2. 02

    Barre, centre work and travelling movement

  3. 03

    Musicality, line and movement quality

  4. 04

    Stage spacing, repertoire and performance etiquette

What learning develops

A class builds more than movement.

  • Posture, flexibility, strength and control
  • Attention to detail and patient self-correction
  • Applying classroom technique to stage repertoire

Class and stage

From daily practice to performance, every step is part of a student’s growth.
