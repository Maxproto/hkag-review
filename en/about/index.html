About Hong Kong Art Groupe

Art is a journey
we take together.

We believe the most valuable outcome of arts education is discovering one’s own potential and learning to make something with others.

Hong Kong Art Groupe has worked in Hong Kong since 1999, creating a platform for young people and arts practitioners to learn through training, performance, competition, workshops and cross-regional exchange.

Our purpose

Rooted in education, open to the world through exchange.

01

Arts education

Progressive training builds technique, aesthetic awareness, discipline and expression.

02

Stage practice

Classroom learning moves into showcases, competitions and public events, where confidence grows through experience.

03

Cultural exchange

Connections across Hong Kong, Mainland China and overseas encourage observation, collaboration and cultural understanding.

04

Community

Art enters district venues and public spaces, bringing students, families and communities together.

“

For many years, our development has been guided by consultation, sharing and building together. We hope to connect experience and resources, learn alongside like-minded partners and create a better environment for the arts in Hong Kong.

Cheung Kwok HungChairman and Director

From the Chairman

Hong Kong Art Groupe will continue to review each activity carefully, seek resources for arts education and welcome ideas and collaboration from practitioners and partners across the community.

Our journey

From one starting point to many cities.

Explore past activities↗

  1. 1999

    Beginning in Hong Kong

    Hong Kong Art Groupe began its arts education and cultural-exchange work, with Mr Cheung Kwok Hung as Chairman and Director.

  2. 2002—2007

    Connecting cities and stages

    Cultural exchanges extended to Sichuan, Zhaoqing, Shanghai, Tianjin, Sydney, Yantai, Nanjing, Harbin, Beijing, Zhongshan, Zhuhai and districts across Hong Kong.

  3. 2009—2012

    Continuing cultural arts festivals

    Hong Kong cultural arts festivals and exchange showcases continued to bring students and arts practitioners from different places together on stage.

  4. 2023—present

    Back in the community, moving forward

    Regular training, district showcases, competitions and public events continue to connect students, families and communities.
