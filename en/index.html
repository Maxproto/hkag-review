Dance training

Four dance styles. Four distinct movement languages.

Each dance style offers a distinct training focus and learning direction. Explore the course, then contact us to find a suitable class.

01

Form · Rhythm · Culture

Chinese Dance

Training draws on Chinese dance foundations, expressive form, rhythm and folk-dance elements to develop coordination, flexibility, strength and cultural understanding. Content is adjusted to each class, age and level.

Explore this course↗
02

Line · Music · Focus

Ballet

Progressive barre and centre work, musicality and stage practice develop posture, focus, control and expressive detail.

Explore this course↗
03

Beat · Energy · Expression

Jazz Dance

Work on timing, coordination, isolations, strength and movement texture, then apply them in combinations and stage pieces.

Explore this course↗
04

Groove · Creativity · Crew

Street Dance

Foundational groove, timing, coordination, strength and creative practice lead into combinations, formations and performance.

About us

Rooted in education since 1999, open to the world through performance.

Hong Kong Art Groupe connects regular training with showcases, competitions, workshops and cultural exchange, so art does not stop at the classroom door.

From following a first beat to completing a stage work with a team, students build technique, focus, collaboration and expression. We also connect arts groups across Hong Kong, Mainland China and overseas, opening wider cultural experience to young people.
